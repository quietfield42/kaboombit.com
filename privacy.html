This website

kaboombit.com and kaboomby.com have no sign-up forms, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising pixels. Our web hosts keep ordinary request records, such as an IP address and the time of a visit.

If you email us asking to hear when the app launches, we keep your email address only to send you that news. Ask us at any time and we will delete it.

The Spot Kaboomby game on kaboomby.com remembers a few things only in your own browser on that device, using the browser's local storage: your best time for each picture, which pictures have already been played, and whether sound is on. None of this is sent to us, there are no names or leaderboards, and you can wipe it with the game's "Reset my scores" button or by clearing your browser's site data. The Kaboomby welcome animation also remembers, for that browser visit only, that it has already played.
