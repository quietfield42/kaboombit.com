Privacy policy

Last updated 16 October 2026 · covers the Kaboombit app, kaboombit.com and kaboomby.com

In short. Families use Kaboombit to turn drawings into short AI-made movies. A parent or guardian holds the account and makes any purchases; children use the app with that adult's supervision. We use drawings and story choices to make the movie you asked for, keep your family's library private, never sell information, show no ads in the app, and never use your child's information for advertising.

Who we are

Kaboombit and Kaboomby are run by THE PET WELLBEING COMPANY PTY LTD (ACN 673 319 652, ABN 13 673 319 652). For anything about privacy, email support@kaboombit.com.

Why we use it

To sign grown-ups in, save and resume projects, make and deliver the movies you ask for, keep your family's private library, check purchases so nobody is charged or credited twice, help when you contact us, keep the service secure, and meet our legal obligations. We do not publish family drawings or movies in any public gallery, and we only use your artwork in marketing if you give separate permission.

Where privacy laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on: performing our contract with you (accounts, movies, purchases and support); our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure and working (technical logs); meeting legal obligations (purchase and tax records); and your consent where we ask for it (for example, using artwork in marketing). You can withdraw consent at any time.

How a drawing becomes a movie

Your drawing and draft start on your device and are saved to your family's private account. When a grown-up approves a movie, our server sends the movie-making service, for each scene, a private link to that one drawing that stops working after one hour, plus that scene's action text inside a fixed instruction we write. It does not send your name, email address, your child's age or your account number. The finished clip is copied back into your family's private library.

The free demo plays movies we made in advance from our own sample drawings; it sends nothing to any movie-making service.

The services involved:

  • Supabase (accounts, database, file storage and server functions). Our project is hosted in Seoul, South Korea (AWS region ap-northeast-2).
  • Higgsfield Inc. (USA) makes the movies, using its own and third-party AI models. Higgsfield's terms say: its service is for adults 18 and over (our company account is the adult account; children never use Higgsfield directly); it may use what it receives and produces to train and improve its AI models unless the customer has an enterprise agreement; it keeps content for 30 days after an account is deleted; and content already used in training can't be removed from trained models. Higgsfield doesn't claim ownership of the movies. This means the drawing photos and story text sent to make a movie, and the movie itself, may be used by Higgsfield to train and improve its AI models. No name, email address, age or account number is sent with them. If that isn't right for your family, you can keep using the free demo, which sends nothing to Higgsfield. If we sign an agreement with Higgsfield that stops this, we will update this policy.
  • Resend sends account emails, such as your sign-in code, from hello@kaboombit.com.
  • Apple and Google handle in-app purchases; our server checks each purchase with them directly to keep credits accurate.
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare host and route our websites. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts website visits without cookies.

So your family's information is stored and processed outside Australia: in South Korea (Supabase) and in the United States (Higgsfield, Resend, Cloudflare, GitHub, Apple and Google). We use these providers only to run Kaboombit, and each is bound by its own security and privacy terms. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses.

Your private library and sharing

Projects and movies belong to the owning grown-up account and your family, not other families. Inside the app, movies play through private links that stop working after one hour; there are no public share links. Right now the only movie that can be saved or shared is our own sample film, and only after a grown-up ticks a box; it uses your phone's share menu. Free demo movies can't be shared. Once a copy has been shared, the person or service that received it controls that copy, and deleting it from Kaboombit cannot bring it back.

This website

kaboombit.com and kaboomby.com have no sign-up forms, no cookies and no advertising pixels. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookies, doesn't build a profile of you and doesn't follow you to other websites; it tells us things like how many people visited and which pages they read. Our web hosts keep ordinary request records, such as an IP address and the time of a visit.

If you email us asking to hear when the app launches, we keep your email address only to send you that news. Ask us at any time and we will delete it.

The Spot Kaboomby game on kaboomby.com remembers a few things only in your own browser on that device, using the browser's local storage: your best time for each picture, which pictures have already been played, and whether sound is on. None of this is sent to us, there are no names or leaderboards, and you can wipe it with the game's "Reset my scores" button or by clearing your browser's site data. The Kaboomby welcome animation also remembers, for that browser visit only, that it has already played.

Advertising

There are no ads, advertising tools, behavioural advertising or sale of family information in the app or for children. We may advertise the app to parents on other platforms. If we ever add anything to our app or websites that collects information for marketing, we will update this policy and ask for any consent needed first.

Camera, photos and microphone

The app asks for the camera or photo library only to add a drawing; you can say no and use another way to add one. If you use "Speak your story", speech is turned into text on your device, with no cloud speech service; the app does not record, save or upload your child's voice, and only the words your child confirms go into the story. A grown-up switches it on with the PIN. We do not ask for contacts or precise location.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Drawings, projects and movies stay in your family's library until you delete them or close the account.

  • Deleting a project: it stops at once, any credit set aside for it comes back, and its files and records are removed by our clean-up run, which runs every 15 minutes, so this usually happens within 15 minutes.
  • Deleting your account: in the app go to Settings → Privacy & your data → Delete account. We stop your projects, remove all your family's files, then delete your account and the family records linked to it (projects, drawings, credits, Child Mode settings). This usually happens within 15 minutes. You can also email support@kaboombit.com from your account's address.
  • What we keep: store purchase records are kept for tax, accounting and dispute reasons with your account removed from them, for 7 years. We also keep an anonymous count of completed deletions.
  • Backups: our hosting keeps daily backups for 7 days, after which deleted information is gone from them too.
  • Copies at Higgsfield follow Higgsfield's terms (see above).

Deleting your account or the app does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in the App Store or Google Play.

Children's privacy

Kaboombit is made for children to enjoy with a parent or guardian, and we take extra care:

  • Only an adult can create an account, approve a movie, buy credits or plans, or save and share a movie.
  • Children never have an account of their own. We don't ask a child for an email address, surname, photo of themselves, location or any way to contact them.
  • We collect only what's needed to make and keep the movies: the drawings, the story choices and an optional nickname.
  • Kaboomby speaks from a fixed script. There is no open chat with an AI, no messaging and no public sharing.
  • There are no ads, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools, and no tracking in the app.

If you believe a child has created an account without an adult, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your family's information, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@kaboombit.com from your account's address. You can also delete projects and your whole account yourself in the app. We aim to answer within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

We use encrypted connections and access controls; each family can only reach its own drawings and movies, and our provider keys stay on our server. No system is completely secure.

Questions and complaints

Email support@kaboombit.com. We will look into it, reply and explain your options. Where Australian privacy law applies you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au); any other local rights you have still apply. In the UK or EU you can contact your local data protection authority, and in the United States the Federal Trade Commission.

Changes

We update this policy when the service or the way we handle information changes, and tell you about important changes.
