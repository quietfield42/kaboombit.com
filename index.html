For grown-ups

Made with you, run by you

A parent or carer looks after the account and anything that needs a grown-up.

  • A grown-up approves each movie before it is made.
  • Kaboomby speaks set lines. There is no open chat.
  • No public feed and no strangers. Movies stay with your family.
What kind of drawings work?

Any drawing on paper: crayon, pencil, pens or paint. Clear, well-lit photos work best.

How long are the movies?

About 15 seconds each. Making one takes a little while, so Kaboomby keeps your child company with a game while you wait.

Does it change my child's drawing?

We aim to keep their characters, lines and colours. Results vary from drawing to drawing, which is why we show real examples.

What happens to the drawings?

They stay in your family's private storage and are sent to an AI video service only to make the movie you asked for. You can delete them at any time. Read our privacy policy.
